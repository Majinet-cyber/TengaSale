Employment Agreement

This Employment Agreement is entered into between TengaSale ("the Company") and the staff member identified above ("the Employee"). It records the current company, role, department and rank details and governs the terms of employment effective from the date stated.

1. Duties and Responsibilities

The Employee agrees to faithfully and diligently perform all duties assigned by the Company in accordance with their designated role and department. Duties include but are not limited to lawful execution of assigned TengaSale workflows, accurate customer and device data handling, responsible task completion, confidentiality maintenance, fraud prevention, and full compliance with the laws of Malawi.

2. Compensation

Performance-based compensation is governed by the current Compensation Annexure A and KPI & Volts Annexure B. Compensation details, volt earnings, and performance bonuses are set out in the accompanying annexures and may be updated from time to time. The compensation cycle runs from the 25th of each calendar month to the 24th of the following month.

3. Conduct and Discipline

Fraud, collusion, misconduct, customer data abuse, device information misuse, or deliberate negligence may trigger formal discipline proceedings, payout lock, suspension, demotion or termination in accordance with Company policy and the applicable laws of Malawi. The Employee has the right to respond, dispute and appeal any disciplinary decision.

4. Confidentiality

The Employee agrees to keep all customer data, financial information, device IMEI details, pricing structures, business processes and Company information strictly confidential during and after the employment period. Breach of confidentiality may result in immediate termination and/or legal action.

5. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement by providing written notice as required by the Malawi Employment Act. The Company reserves the right to summarily dismiss for gross misconduct. On termination, the Employee must return all Company property, devices, and credentials.

6. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Malawi.

Confidentiality & Data Protection Agreement

This Agreement is entered into between TengaSale and the staff member identified above and forms part of the employment relationship.

1. Scope of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" includes all customer data, device IMEI numbers, financial records, pricing structures, business processes, system designs, software, source code, strategic plans, staff records, merchant data, contract details, credit assessments, and any other information the Employee accesses in the course of their duties.

2. Obligations

The Employee agrees to:

(a) Keep all Confidential Information strictly private and not disclose it to any unauthorised person;

(b) Use Confidential Information solely for authorised Company purposes;

(c) Report any suspected breach or unauthorised access to management immediately;

(d) Comply with all applicable data protection laws, including Malawi's Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act.

3. Duration

This obligation applies during the employment relationship and continues indefinitely after its termination, regardless of the reason for termination.

4. Breach

Breach of this Agreement may result in immediate termination, disciplinary proceedings, civil claims for damages, and/or criminal prosecution under applicable Malawi law.

5. Return of Information

On termination of employment, the Employee must immediately return or permanently delete all Confidential Information in their possession and certify in writing that they have done so.
