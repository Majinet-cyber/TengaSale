{# /frd-panel connectivity #} {# Connectivity status reference guide #}
✓ Paid but Offline

Customer has paid. Device just needs to come online to sync. Action: Ask customer to connect to mobile data or Wi-Fi for 5–10 minutes.

⚠ Offline + Arrears

Customer is overdue and device is offline. Monitor as risk signal. Action: Attempt customer contact. If offline continues, escalate.

⏳ Sync Pending

A lock or unlock command is queued but device is offline. Action: Command pending. Device must come online before status can update.

🔴 Possible Evasion

Extended offline with arrears — possible connectivity anomaly. Not confirmed fraud. Escalate for HQ review.

↩ Device Came Online

Device reconnected after many offline days. Action: Refresh lock/payment status and verify customer activity.
