Open Roles

We are hiring now

We hire on capability and character, not just credentials. If you are excellent at what you do, we want to hear from you.

Merchant Administrators

Support merchant onboarding, verify shops, collect documents, GPS details, and help partners operate smoothly on the TengaSale platform.

Underwriters

Review customer applications, verify KYC, call customers and guarantors, and protect portfolio quality with sound credit decisions.

Customer Support

Help customers with payments, contracts, locked devices, and account questions. First line of TengaSale's customer experience.

Field Operations

Visit merchants, verify shops, support device onboarding, and resolve operational issues on the ground across Malawi.

Software / Data Talent

Help build dashboards, integrations, credit scoring, fraud detection, and reporting tools that drive platform intelligence.
