The Process

How TengaSale works

A straightforward six-step process from application to full device ownership — handled through your merchant.

1
Choose a device
Browse available smartphone models at a participating TengaSale merchant and select the right fit for your needs and budget.
2
Apply through a merchant
Complete a financing application in-store. Your merchant submits the request and supporting documents through the TengaSale platform.
3
Verify your identity
Present your Malawi National ID for identity verification. KYC is completed through the merchant as part of the structured application process.
4
Pay your deposit
Pay an initial deposit to activate your contract. The deposit amount is agreed at the point of application based on device and repayment term.
5
Activate your contract
Once approved and your deposit is confirmed, your device contract is activated and your repayment schedule is issued.
6
Repay over time
Make structured payments via Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba. Settle early to reduce your remaining balance — the sooner you pay, the more you save.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Answers to common questions about TengaSale, repayments, and contracts.

How do I make a payment?

Visit tengasale.africa/pay/ and search for your contract using your contract number, PayG number, National ID, or registered phone number. Your contract details will appear. Select your payment amount and mobile money provider, then follow the steps to complete the payment.

Can I repay early?

Yes. TengaSale supports early settlement with discounts on your remaining balance. When you view your contract, early settlement options are shown where available. Settling sooner means you pay less overall — the discount percentage depends on your term and remaining balance.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Your contract will be marked as overdue after the due date passes. Depending on your contract terms and device configuration, an overdue contract may result in restrictions on device use. Contact your merchant or TengaSale support as soon as possible if you are unable to make a scheduled payment.

Do I need ID to apply?

Yes. A valid Malawi National ID is required to complete a financing application. Your identity information is used for KYC verification and is stored securely as part of your contract record. See our Privacy Policy for details on how your data is handled.

Can a merchant see my contract details?

Your merchant has access to the contracts they have created through TengaSale, including your repayment status and contract progress. This is necessary for them to support you and manage their portfolio. Merchant access is role-controlled and all actions are audit-logged.

How do I become a TengaSale merchant?

Submit a merchant interest form at tengasale.africa/site/merchant-signup/. Our onboarding team will contact you to discuss requirements and next steps. Once onboarded, you will receive access to the merchant platform. See our Merchant Terms for full details.

Is TengaSale available outside Malawi?

TengaSale is currently operating as a Malawi pilot. Expansion to other markets is planned as the platform matures. If you are interested in partnering in another market, contact us at support@tengasale.africa.

Which payment methods are supported?

TengaSale supports Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba for contract repayments. Customers can pay directly through the TengaSale payment portal using their mobile money account — no bank account required. Contact support for details on your specific contract payment options.

Who do I contact for help?

For payment and contract queries, email support@tengasale.africa or visit the Support page. You can also contact your merchant directly if your query relates to your original application or device.
