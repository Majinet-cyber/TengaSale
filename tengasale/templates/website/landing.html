Frequently asked questions

Before you apply.

The essentials about applications, payments, protection and working with Tenga.

Does Tenga offer solar home systems?

Tenga is preparing Solar PayGo products. Availability, packages and pricing will be announced when ready. Contact Tenga about solar.

How do I get a phone through Tenga?

Choose a phone, submit your application, complete identity and affordability checks, then receive the confirmed deposit and payment plan.

How much is the deposit?

Deposits and instalments depend on the phone and final assessment. Website prices are illustrative; Tenga confirms the exact amount before you commit.

Can I pay daily, weekly or monthly?

You can explore daily, weekly and monthly payments. Your confirmed agreement shows the cadence and amount that apply to you.

What happens if a payment is late?

Tenga sends reminders and provides a way to pay through mobile money. Device protection may apply under your agreement, with access restored after a confirmed payment.

What if my payment is not showing?

Keep the transaction reference, check the payment gateway, and contact support under Payment help if it still has not reflected.

How can my business become a Tenga merchant?

Use the merchant partnership option below or complete the existing merchant onboarding form. The team will review your business details and follow up.

How do I contact Tenga about careers, media or investment?

Select Careers or Investor or media in the support form. Your enquiry is addressed to the same public support team for routing.
