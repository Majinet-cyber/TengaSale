Key Legal Terms

  1. Ownership: The device remains the property of TengaSale until all payments are fully settled.
  2. Default: Failure to pay within 14 consecutive days constitutes a default event.
  3. Repossession: After 30 consecutive days of default, TengaSale may repossess the device.
  4. Device restrictions: TengaSale may restrict device access if payments are overdue, fraud is suspected, or a material breach occurs.
  5. Loss or theft: Losing, damaging, or selling the device does not cancel the payment obligation. Notify TengaSale within 72 hours of loss/theft.
  6. No replacement: TengaSale does not replace lost or stolen devices.
  7. Deposit required: Deposit must be paid before final activation.
  8. Electronic acceptance: Electronic signatures, OTP, and digital acceptance are legally binding under the laws of Malawi.
  9. Payment allocation: Payments are allocated first to recovery costs, then overdue amounts, then fees, then current instalments, then principal.
  10. Data processing: TengaSale processes personal data for contract administration, fraud prevention, and compliance.
