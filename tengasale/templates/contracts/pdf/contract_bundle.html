C. Core Legal Terms

C1. Ownership. Ownership of the Device remains with TengaSale Digital Finance until all amounts due under this Agreement have been fully paid and TengaSale records ownership transfer. Until then, the Customer has conditional possession and use only.
C2. Default. Failure to keep payments current after the paid-through date constitutes an Event of Default.
C3. Repossession. If the Customer remains in default for a prolonged period, including 30 days or more after required payment becomes overdue, TengaSale may initiate recovery or repossession of the Device, subject to this Agreement and applicable law.
C4. Resale. If the Device is repossessed, TengaSale may inspect, refurbish, and resell the Device. Net resale proceeds may be applied to the Customer's outstanding balance. Any shortfall or surplus shall be handled according to TengaSale policy and applicable law.
C5. Device restriction. TengaSale may restrict access to the device where payments are overdue, fraud is suspected, or a material breach occurs.
C6. Electronic acceptance. Electronic signatures, OTP, and digital acceptance are legally binding under the laws of Malawi.
C7. Payment allocation. Payments are allocated: (1) recovery costs, (2) overdue amounts, (3) fees, (4) current instalments, (5) principal.
C8. Recovery costs. Any lawful repossession, tracing, legal or recovery costs incurred by TengaSale may be charged to the Customer to the extent permitted by law.
C9. Expanded recovery costs. If the Customer defaults and TengaSale incurs lawful, reasonable, and documented repossession, tracing, legal, recovery, storage, repair, or resale-related costs, such costs may be added to the Customer's outstanding balance to the extent permitted by applicable law and this Agreement.

D1. IMEI verification. Customer consents to device IMEI verification before activation.
