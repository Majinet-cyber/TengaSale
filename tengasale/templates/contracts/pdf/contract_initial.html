C. OWNERSHIP.

The financed device shall remain the property of TengaSale Digital Finance ("TengaSale") until the Total Contract Price has been paid in full by the customer. No title, interest, or ownership rights shall pass to the customer until full payment is confirmed by TengaSale.

D. CUSTOMER CONSENT.

By accepting this agreement, the customer consents to: (i) purchasing the device on an instalment basis as described above; (ii) TengaSale retaining ownership of the device until fully paid; (iii) TengaSale monitoring repayment status and applying device access restrictions in accordance with this agreement; and (iv) the customer's repayment obligations as set out herein.

E. DEFAULT.

The customer shall be considered in default if any instalment payment remains unpaid for 14 consecutive days from its due date. Upon default, TengaSale reserves the right to restrict access to the device and/or pursue recovery of the outstanding balance.

F. REPOSSESSION.

If the customer remains in default for 30 or more consecutive days, TengaSale may repossess the financed device. Repossession may be carried out by TengaSale or its authorised agents. The customer shall cooperate with any lawful repossession process.

G. DISPOSAL OF REPOSSESSED DEVICE.

Following repossession, TengaSale may sell or otherwise dispose of the repossessed device to recover the outstanding balance. Any proceeds in excess of the outstanding amount, after deducting reasonable costs of repossession and disposal, shall be returned to the customer.

H. ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE.

The customer acknowledges that an OTP verification code, biometric confirmation, touchscreen signature, or any form of electronic acceptance (including acceptance via SMS, WhatsApp, or electronic form) constitutes a valid and legally binding signature for the purposes of this agreement. The customer's electronic acceptance of this agreement is equivalent to a handwritten signature.

I. IMEI AND DEVICE VERIFICATION.

The customer acknowledges that the financed smartphone is identified by its IMEI and/or serial number as recorded in this agreement. TengaSale may verify the device identity at any time for fraud prevention, contract administration, and device-lock readiness. Any discrepancy in IMEI may result in contract suspension pending investigation.

J. DEVICE LOCK AND ACCESS RESTRICTION.

The customer acknowledges that the device may be enrolled in a device-lock or device-management system operated by TengaSale or a third-party provider. If payments are overdue in accordance with this agreement and applicable law, TengaSale may restrict access to the device remotely until the account is brought up to date or fully settled. Device unlocking will follow upon payment confirmation.

K. PAYMENT ALLOCATION AND RECONCILIATION.

All payments made by the customer shall be applied to the outstanding contract balance and recorded in TengaSale's systems. Payment confirmation may depend on mobile money or payment provider reconciliation timelines. The customer is advised to retain payment receipts.
