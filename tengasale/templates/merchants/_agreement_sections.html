1

Appointment and Participation

TengaSale appoints the Merchant to submit customer applications for smartphone financing through the TengaSale platform. This appointment is non-exclusive and revocable at TengaSale's discretion.

  • The Merchant is not guaranteed approval of every application submitted.
  • TengaSale retains full discretion over approvals, rejections, pricing, product availability, customer eligibility, device status, commissions, and all operational controls.
  • This Agreement does not create an employment, partnership, joint venture, agency, or franchise relationship.
2

Good Faith and Honest Conduct

The Merchant must at all times act in good faith and with integrity. The Merchant must not:

  • Submit fake, misleading, manipulated, or incomplete customer information.
  • Coach customers to provide false or exaggerated information.
  • Dishonestly split or misrepresent customer deposits.
  • Knowingly submit customers who are unlikely or unable to repay.
  • Misuse customer documents, photographs, or personal data.
  • Take any action that may damage TengaSale's reputation or business interests.
3

Good Customer Selection

The Merchant agrees to use reasonable judgment and care when selecting customers to submit for financing. The Merchant should consider:

  • Customer identity — genuine, verifiable, and their own.
  • Willingness and genuine intention to repay.
  • Stable and verifiable income or livelihood.
  • A reliable, reachable phone number.
  • A traceable, accurate home or work address.
  • Good reputation and trustworthy character within the community.
  • Realistic ability to afford the agreed monthly repayment.
  • Honesty and transparency during the application process.
  • Full awareness of and agreement to repayment obligations.

The Merchant must not knowingly submit customers who:

  • Are unreachable or have provided false contact details.
  • Are using another person's identity documents.
  • Have no realistic ability to repay.
  • Display suspicious or dishonest behaviour during the process.
  • Do not understand or refuse to acknowledge their instalment obligations.
  • Refuse to cooperate with KYC or identity verification.
4

Customer Consent and KYC

The Merchant must obtain clear, informed consent from each customer before capturing or uploading any personal data, including:

  • Identity documents (national ID, passport).
  • Facial photographs and selfies.
  • GPS location and address details.
  • Phone numbers of the customer, neighbours, and guarantors.
  • Customer signature.

The Merchant must explain to each customer that their information will be used for financing assessment, identity verification, underwriting, repayment tracking, SMS communication, and contract enforcement. The Merchant must not upload any data without the customer's informed consent.

5

Accuracy of Information

The Merchant is responsible for ensuring that all information submitted through the platform is accurate, complete, and current. The Merchant must:

  • Correct errors immediately upon becoming aware of them.
  • Cooperate fully when TengaSale or an underwriter requests clarification, additional documents, or retakes of KYC images.

Repeated submission of inaccurate, incomplete, or low-quality applications may result in suspension of the Merchant account.

6

TengaSale Discretion

TengaSale may, at its sole and absolute discretion:

  • Approve or reject any application without being required to give reasons.
  • Request additional information or verification at any stage.
  • Suspend the Merchant account during any investigation.
  • Adjust the deals, pricing, or products available to the Merchant.
  • Amend commission rules with reasonable notice where practicable.
  • Withhold commissions during active investigations.
  • Reverse or claw back commissions connected to fraud, defaults caused by Merchant misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.
  • Terminate Merchant access for cause or for operational reasons.
  • Block or cancel suspicious or incomplete contracts.
  • Change operational policies, procedures, or rules.
7

Commissions and Settlement

Merchant commissions are earned only in accordance with TengaSale's active commission schedule, which may be updated from time to time. Commission may remain pending until all conditions set out in TengaSale's rules are satisfied.

TengaSale may withhold, reduce, reverse, or claw back commission in the event of:

  • Fraud or misrepresentation by the Merchant or customer.
  • Customer default arising from Merchant misconduct or poor customer selection.
  • Duplicate, cancelled, or disputed applications or contracts.
  • Breach of this Agreement.

The right to commission does not override TengaSale's right to reject or reverse suspicious activity.

8

Customer Payments

  • The Merchant must direct customers to use TengaSale's official and approved payment channels only.
  • The Merchant must not collect customer repayment instalments unless explicitly and formally authorised in writing by TengaSale.
  • The Merchant must not misappropriate, redirect, or withhold customer payments.
  • The Merchant must not make payment promises or commitments to customers outside the official TengaSale contract terms.
9

Device and Contract Rules

  • The Merchant must not release devices to customers outside the approved TengaSale workflow.
  • IMEI and device details must be handled strictly in accordance with the TengaSale process.
  • Device locking and PAYG (Pay-As-You-Go) controls are managed entirely by TengaSale.
  • The Merchant must not interfere with device locking, payment reminders, or enforcement mechanisms in any way.
10

Fraud, Abuse, and Investigations

Fraud includes, but is not limited to:

  • Submission of fake customers or fabricated applications.
  • Use of forged, borrowed, or altered identity documents.
  • Manipulated KYC photographs or biometric data.
  • False addresses, income figures, or employment information.
  • Collusion with customers to deceive TengaSale.
  • Unauthorised release of financed devices.
  • Misdirection or misappropriation of customer payments.
  • Submission of duplicate or ghost applications.
  • Inflation or falsification of sales records.

TengaSale may investigate suspected fraud and may suspend Merchant account access while any investigation is ongoing. Investigation results may be reported to relevant authorities.

11

Data Protection and Confidentiality

  • The Merchant must protect the personal data of all customers in accordance with applicable law.
  • The Merchant must not share customer documents or information outside the authorised TengaSale process.
  • The Merchant must keep confidential all TengaSale business rules, pricing, underwriting criteria, platform access credentials, and customer data.
  • Confidentiality obligations survive the termination of this Agreement.
12

Platform Access

  • The Merchant account is specific to the registered business and its authorised representative.
  • Login credentials must not be shared carelessly or given to unauthorised persons.
  • The Merchant is fully responsible for all activity conducted under their account.
  • TengaSale may disable access at any time for security reasons, suspected breach, or investigation.
13

Training and Cooperation

The Merchant agrees to:

  • Follow TengaSale's instructions, training materials, operational guidelines, and workflow updates.
  • Cooperate fully with underwriters, HQ staff, support teams, and investigation teams.
  • Keep up to date with changes to TengaSale's policies and platform features.
14

No Guarantee of Income

TengaSale does not guarantee any minimum sales volume, number of approvals, or level of commission income. The Merchant's earnings depend entirely on approved and completed contracts, customer repayment performance, TengaSale's commission rules, and the Merchant's compliance with this Agreement.

15

Suspension and Termination

TengaSale may suspend or terminate Merchant access for any of the following reasons:

  • Fraud or attempted fraud.
  • Repeated submission of poor-quality or inaccurate applications.
  • High customer default patterns attributable to Merchant conduct.
  • Misuse of customer personal data.
  • Breach of any provision of this Agreement.
  • Conduct causing reputational harm to TengaSale.
  • Failure to cooperate with investigations or audits.
  • Any illegal activity connected to the Merchant's participation.
  • Abuse of the platform or its systems.
16

Limitation of Authority

The Merchant is not an employee, partner, legal agent, or representative of TengaSale, and has no authority to bind TengaSale in any way beyond submitting financing applications through the platform. The Merchant must not make promises, commitments, or representations on behalf of TengaSale to customers or any third party.

17

Dispute Resolution

In the event of any dispute arising from this Agreement, the parties shall first attempt to resolve the matter in good faith through direct discussion. If no resolution is reached, the matter shall be referred to mediation or such other process as TengaSale may determine. 18

Acceptance and Signature

By signing this Agreement (electronically or otherwise), the Merchant confirms that they have read and understood the entire Agreement, agree to act in good faith, will select customers responsibly, understand that TengaSale retains full operational discretion, understand that commissions may be withheld or reversed for misconduct or fraud, and consent to the use of an electronic signature and digital records as legally binding.
